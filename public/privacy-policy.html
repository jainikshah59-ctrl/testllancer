Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Collancer (“Collancer”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) connects brands and creators for influencer and content collaborations. This policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide when you register or contact us, including your name, email address, phone number, social profile information, creator or brand details, campaign preferences, and other information you choose to submit.

We may also collect technical information such as device type, browser information, approximate location, IP address, and website usage information for security, analytics, and performance.

2. How we use information

We use information to provide and improve Collancer, create and manage creator and brand profiles, facilitate collaboration discovery, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, maintain security, communicate service updates, and improve our products and AI-assisted features.

3. Sharing of information

We may share relevant profile or campaign information with other Collancer users when necessary to facilitate a collaboration. We may also use trusted technology, hosting, analytics, communication, and infrastructure providers to operate the service.

We do not sell personal information as a standalone product.

4. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so please avoid submitting passwords, financial credentials, or other highly sensitive information through public forms.

5. Cookies and analytics

Collancer may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to remember preferences, understand usage, improve performance, and protect the service.

6. Your choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information that we hold, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

7. Children

Collancer is intended for users who are legally able to use the service. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Collancer evolves. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email support@collancer.in.
